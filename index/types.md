### **the fundemental types**  
---
internally variables (the `mindustry.logic.LVar` class), are composed of their `Double` (number) value, and their `Object` value  
internally, a boolean decides whether the Object or Number value is used

the types that can fill the `Object` value are:  
`Building`, `Team`, `Boolean`, `String`, `Null`, `Enum` (internally), and `Content`
